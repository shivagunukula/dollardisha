PRIVACY

Your research stays yours.

Last updated: September 2026. This privacy notice explains the information DollarDisha may process when you use the website.

Information you provide

If you create an account or contact support, we may receive your email address, display name and messages. Watchlists, custom indices, saved screens, portfolios, alerts and notes may be stored so the features work across sessions.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, provide saved research features, improve reliability and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use your research to provide personalised investment advice.

Cookies and local storage

DollarDisha uses essential session mechanisms and browser storage for preferences and locally saved research tools. We may use limited operational logs for security and reliability. We do not use non-essential advertising cookies.

Third-party services

Authentication, hosting, payment and market-data services may process limited technical or account information to provide their services. Provider data is subject to the relevant provider terms and privacy policies. Market-data API keys are kept server-side.

Retention, deletion and export

Account and saved-research data is retained while needed to provide the service. You can request account deletion or an export of your saved research by contacting support. We may retain limited records where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We use contractual and technical safeguards appropriate to the service and the information involved.

Your choices

You may browse public research without an account, update your account details, sign out, or contact us about access, correction or deletion requests. We will verify requests before changing account data.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@dollardisha.in.
