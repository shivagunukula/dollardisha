Data sources

  • Market quotes, profiles and fundamentals are supplied through configured market-data providers.
  • Company filings and filing links are sourced from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission where available.
  • Provider coverage, exchange support, update frequency and licensing can vary by symbol and plan.

Calculations

Ratios are displayed only when the required reported inputs are available. DollarDisha does not invent missing values; a dash means the provider did not return a reliable value for that field.

  • P/E = current share price ÷ diluted EPS (TTM), when EPS is positive and reported.
  • Free cash flow = operating cash flow − capital expenditure.
  • FCF yield = free cash flow ÷ market capitalisation.
  • Growth compares the latest reported period with the matching prior period; CAGR uses the first and last reported values across the selected years.
  • INR context = USD value × latest available USD/INR reference rate; it excludes taxes, fees and broker spreads.

Freshness labels

  • Updated HH:MM means the application received a provider response at that time.
  • Latest available means a quote was returned without a provider timestamp.
  • Provider unavailable means no configured source returned a reliable value for that field.
  • Not reported means the company or filing did not disclose the requested figure.

Fallback order

For market data, DollarDisha prefers configured FMP and Twelve Data feeds, then uses Nasdaq or public-market fallback coverage where available. SEC EDGAR remains the source for issuer filings. Fallback values are labelled in the research status view.

Timing and accuracy

Quotes may be delayed, cached or unavailable during provider outages, market closures or rate limits. Always check the timestamp and the original filing before making a decision.
