DATA & METHODOLOGY

Transparent by design.

DollarDisha combines provider market data with primary-source company information so researchers can understand where a number comes from.

Data sources

  • Market quotes, profiles and fundamentals are supplied through configured market-data providers.
  • Company filings and filing links are sourced from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission where available.
  • Provider coverage, exchange support, update frequency and licensing can vary by symbol and plan.

Calculations

Ratios such as P/E, price-to-book, returns and growth are displayed when the required reported inputs are available. We do not invent missing values; a dash means the provider did not return a reliable value for that field.

Timing and accuracy

Quotes may be delayed, cached or unavailable during provider outages, market closures or rate limits. Always check the timestamp and the original filing before making a decision.

DollarDisha is a research and education tool, not investment advice. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.
