ABOUT DOLLARDISHA

Research US markets with clarity.

DollarDisha is a research workspace for Indian investors studying companies listed in the United States. It brings market quotes, screening, company fundamentals, charts, SEC filings, watchlists and comparisons into one focused place.

DollarDisha is built for research and education. It is not a broker, exchange, investment adviser or trading platform.

What we are building

Our goal is to make company research easier to repeat: start with a question, inspect the numbers, read the original filings, compare alternatives and save the work for later.

Independent research

DollarDisha does not recommend securities or execute trades. Users should verify important information against issuer filings, exchange data and their own professional advisers.
